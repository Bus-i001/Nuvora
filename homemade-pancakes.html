Homemade Pancakes (Fluffy and Easy)

Food & Cooking · Updated 23 November 2026

These pancakes use simple pantry ingredients and come together in about 25 minutes. The batter is easy to mix, and a short rest gives you soft, fluffy results every time.

Homemade Pancakes

Makes8 pancakes (serves 4)
Prep time10 min
Cook time15 min
HeatMedium (stovetop)

Ingredients

  • 200 g (1 1/2 cups) all-purpose (plain) flour
  • 2 tbsp (25 g) sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 300 ml (1 1/4 cups) milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) butter, melted, plus extra for the pan

Method

  1. Mix the dry ingredients. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Mix the wet ingredients. In a jug, whisk the milk, egg and melted butter.
  3. Combine. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir until just combined. A few small lumps are fine. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
  4. Heat the pan. Warm a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add a little butter.
  5. Cook. Pour in about 1/4 cup (60 ml) of batter for each pancake. Cook for about 2 minutes, until bubbles form and the edges look set.
  6. Flip. Turn the pancake over and cook for 1 to 2 minutes more, until golden. Repeat with the remaining batter.

Tips and variations

  • Don't overmix. Overmixed batter makes tougher pancakes.
  • Adjust the heat. If they brown too fast but stay raw inside, lower the heat.
  • Add flavour. Stir in 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, or top with berries or sliced banana.
  • Keep them warm. Place cooked pancakes on a plate in a low oven, around 90 °C (200 °F), while you finish the batch.

Storing and reheating

Cool pancakes completely, then keep them in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. For longer storage, freeze them with a piece of baking paper between each one for up to 2 months. Reheat in a toaster or microwave until hot all the way through.
