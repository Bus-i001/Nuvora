Tablet vs Laptop: Which Should You Choose?

Technology · Updated 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

The short answer: choose a laptop if you type a lot or need full computer software. Choose a tablet if you mostly read, watch, draw or browse and want something light.

Side by side

FeatureTabletLaptop
Best forReading, video, drawing, browsingWriting, study, work, multitasking
TypingOn-screen, or with an extra keyboardBuilt-in keyboard and trackpad
PortabilityVery light and thinHeavier but still portable
SoftwareMobile appsFull desktop programs
Touch screenYes, and pen support on many modelsOnly on some models
Storage and portsUsually limitedUsually more options
Total costAccessories like a keyboard can add upEverything is included

Tablets

Pros

  • Light and easy to carry or hold
  • Great for reading, video and games
  • Touch and pen input suit drawing and note-taking
  • Simple to use, with fast start-up

Cons

  • Typing long documents is slower without a keyboard
  • Multitasking is more limited
  • Some desktop programs are not available

Laptops

Pros

  • Comfortable keyboard and trackpad for long work
  • Runs full programs and handles many windows at once
  • More ports and storage options

Cons

  • Heavier and bulkier than a tablet
  • Less comfortable for reading or relaxing in bed
  • Battery life varies between models

Our recommendation

Choose a laptop if you write essays or reports, use programs like full spreadsheets or design software, or need one device for school or work.

Choose a tablet if you mainly stream video, read, browse, draw or take notes, and want something you can carry everywhere.

Can't decide? Many people choose a laptop for work and add a tablet for fun later. A 2-in-1 laptop with a detachable or folding screen is another option worth looking at.

Questions to ask before you buy

  • What will I use it for most days?
  • Do I need to type for long periods?
  • Which programs or apps must it run?
  • What is my total budget, including accessories?
