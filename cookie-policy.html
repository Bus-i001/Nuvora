Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Nuvora ("we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They help a site work properly, remember your choices, measure how it is used and, in some cases, show relevant advertising.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies. Needed for the site to work, such as remembering your cookie choice or keeping your shop basket.
  • Analytics cookies. Help us understand which pages are read and how people find us. We only use these if you agree, where the law requires it.
  • Advertising cookies. Used by Google AdSense and its partners to show ads and measure how they perform. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your choice and location.
  • Affiliate cookies. When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may set a cookie so a sale can be credited to us. This costs you nothing extra.

Third parties

Some cookies are set by third parties, including Google, affiliate networks and retailers, and the payment and checkout providers for our digital shop. These companies have their own privacy and cookie policies, and we do not control them.

Your choices

  • Cookie banner. Where required, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies when you first visit, and change your mind at any time.
  • Browser settings. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Some parts of the site may not work properly if you block all cookies.
  • Google ads. Manage personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com.
  • General guidance. See allaboutcookies.org.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our site and the services we use change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
